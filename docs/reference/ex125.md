# Example 1.2.5: two drugs at two doses in a split-plot design

Average packed cell volume (PCV, %) of animals in six regions; in each
region one herd received Berenil and one Samorin (main plots), and
within a herd one group of animals received the high and one the low
dose (subplots) (Table 1.2.5 and Appendix A of the book).

## Usage

``` r
ex125
```

## Format

A data frame with 24 rows and 4 variables:

- Region:

  region: 1 to 6.

- Drug:

  drug: `BERENIL` or `samorin`.

- dose:

  dose: `h` (high) or `l` (low).

- Pcv:

  average PCV.

## Source

Duchateau, L., Janssen, P. and Rowlands, G. J. (1998). *Linear Mixed
Models. An Introduction with Applications in Veterinary Research*.
International Livestock Research Institute, Nairobi. Appendix A.
