# Example 3.1: two drugs and their doses, herds nested in drug (part of the data)

Packed cell volume (PCV, %) at treatment and one month later; Berenil
(at two doses) and Samorin (one dose) were given to animals in herds
nested in drug. The book prints only part of the data (a few animals per
herd and dose); these 38 rows are that part, so the analyses of Example
3.1 run but do not reproduce the printed results.

## Usage

``` r
ex31
```

## Format

A data frame with 38 rows and 6 variables:

- herd:

  herd within drug: 1 to 4 (herds with the same number but another drug
  are different herds).

- animal_id:

  animal.

- PCV1:

  PCV at treatment.

- PCV2:

  PCV one month after treatment.

- dose:

  dose within drug: 1 or 2 (Samorin has dose 1 only).

- drug:

  drug: `BERENIL` or `SAMORIN`.

## Source

Duchateau, L., Janssen, P. and Rowlands, G. J. (1998). *Linear Mixed
Models. An Introduction with Applications in Veterinary Research*.
International Livestock Research Institute, Nairobi. Appendix A.
